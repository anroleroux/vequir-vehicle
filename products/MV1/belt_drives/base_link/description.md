The base link (vertical) of the belt drive's four-bar linkage. It has one
hinge at the bottom middle, then goes up and splits in two, forming an
upside-down triangle. The two top ends go up a little, to the two sides of
the top hinge, and extend diagonally, laterally and up some more, to a
joint each for a shock absorber.

A bearing for the drive shaft sits in it, in the middle between the lateral
links.

The joints are kept basic for now, to be refined later.
