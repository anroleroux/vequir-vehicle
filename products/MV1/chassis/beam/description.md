A beam that runs in the longitudinal direction (along x, front to back) of
the chassis.

At each end of the beam is a disc, its centre line in z (the disc lies
flat), with a hole through its middle. The lateral beams' discs support the
beam under these discs, their centred pins in the holes.

A slot across the beam's underside, at its middle, holds the sliding beam
against the beam and guides it sideways.
