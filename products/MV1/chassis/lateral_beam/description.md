A lateral beam (along y, sideways), shorter than the longitudinal beam,
with a disc on top of it and a pin sticking up from the disc's middle. Two
of them support the longitudinal beam: each disc carries one of the
longitudinal beam's end discs, its centred pin in that disc's hole. They
are the steering beams.

A middle T leg extends from each steering beam in the direction of the
longitudinal beam, to steer by.
