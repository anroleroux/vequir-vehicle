A plate in the x-z plane, with a hinge at its top and at its bottom. Two of
them, facing each other, are the fixed and the moving plate of the
four-bar linkage.

It extends up and out over the top arm's hole; the shock absorber's upper
joint is there.

Below its bottom hinge it carries the bracket of the wheel's drive motor, a
standard small DC motor tilted downwards towards the hub.

Its upper link is shifted down 4 mm and its lower link up 4 mm, to reduce
the overall height of the suspension.

Its upper link is lowered another 4 mm.
