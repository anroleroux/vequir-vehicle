A plate hinged at one end to the fixed plate and at the other to the moving
plate. Two of them, one at the top and one at the bottom, complete the
four-bar linkage.

Its legs at the wheel side are extended, so there is more space to get a
shock absorber through, and the legs are angled so that the H is wider at
the wheel side. The corners of the H's cross link are filleted.
