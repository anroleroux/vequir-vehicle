The wheel-side vertical link of the four-bar linkage: the linkage plate,
extended down at the bottom. The wheel will come there.

It is as wide as the arms' angled legs leave room for at the wheel side.

Below the bottom arm, a joint on its inner face carries the shock absorber's
lower end.

It carries the wheel hub, on a bearing boss on its outer face, lower down
the extension.

The shock absorber's lower joint is level with the bottom link, and the
hub is moved up by the same.
