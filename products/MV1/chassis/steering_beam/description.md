A lateral steering beam (along y, sideways), with a disc on top of it and a
pin sticking up from the disc's middle. Two of them carry the main beam:
each disc carries one of its round ends, the pin in that end's hole, so the
steering beam turns about it.

A middle T leg extends from each steering beam in the direction of the main
beam, to steer by. The ends of the steering beams extend downward into
uprights, the beam's normal width, each carrying a linkage.

The steering beam is shorter laterally (shorter again since), and built from plates rather than
beams: a plate at the top and at the outer sides (the uprights' outer
faces), with curved gussets on each side edge that run along the entire
beam and its extensions.

The steering extension has a slot along it, with a slider that the servo
horn's pin goes into.

Its downward extensions are shortened: the suspension assemblies are moved
up about 30 % of the base link's height, and another 10 mm.

It is split into two: the rear steering beam is this one, and the front
steering beam adds the cab's extrusions to it.

Its pivot cylinders (the discs the base plate sits on) are lowered as far
as the servo lever connection allows, so the base plate is lowered too.
