# Linkage

A four-bar linkage of plates that moves in the y-z plane: an upright plate
in the x-z plane, hinged at its top and bottom to two arm plates, which are
hinged at their far ends to a fourth plate, parallel to the first: the
wheel link, extended down at the bottom for the wheel.

The arms at the top and bottom are H links (the two outer knuckles of each
hinge); the plates at the sides are I links (the middle knuckle).

A shock absorber runs between the steering beam and the bottom arm: through
the bottom arm, to a joint on the wheel link below the bottom arm.

A standard small DC motor for each suspension, tilted downwards, drives the
wheel hub on the wheel link through a shaft and couplers.
