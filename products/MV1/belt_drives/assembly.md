# Belt drives

A second suspension concept for the MV1, a sibling of the drives: again a
four-bar linkage that moves in the y-z plane, between an upright base link
and an upright wheel link.

The base link has one hinge at the bottom middle, goes up, and splits in
two, forming an upside-down triangle; its two top ends go up a little to
the two sides of the top hinge, and extend diagonally, laterally and up,
to a joint each for a shock absorber. The top link is a single flat bar.
The bottom link is a double bar link with an H profile. The wheel link
extends down past the bottom link, to have the wheel hub below the bottom
link.

The drive shaft will go between bearings in the vertical links, in the
middle between the lateral links. Belt drives will connect the shaft's ends
with the motor on the base link and with the hub at the wheel end. Two
shock absorbers will go from the base link's top extensions to joints on
the bottom link.

The lateral links are extended by 300 %, the top link's width is halved,
and the necks of the Ys are closed up flat.

For now only the links are designed, with basic joints, to be refined
later.
