# Drives

One drive at each corner of the MV1: a four-bar linkage of plates that
moves in the y-z plane, an upright plate in the x-z plane (the base link)
hinged at its top and bottom to two arm plates, hinged at their far ends to
the wheel link, parallel to the first, extended down at the bottom for the
wheel.

The arms at the top and bottom are H links (the two outer knuckles of each
hinge); the plates at the sides are I links (the middle knuckle).

A shock absorber runs from the base link's joint over the top arm, through
both arms, to a joint on the wheel link below the bottom arm.

A standard small DC motor for each suspension, tilted downwards, drives the
wheel hub on the wheel link through a shaft and couplers. The wheel is
bolted to the hub's flange.

The upper horizontal links are shifted down 4 mm and the lower ones up
4 mm, to reduce the overall height of the suspension.
